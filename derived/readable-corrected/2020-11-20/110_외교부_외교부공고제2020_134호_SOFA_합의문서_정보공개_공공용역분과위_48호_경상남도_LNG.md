2020년 11월 20일

(단위: 원/MJ, 부가세 윜앸)

동 합의는 한･미 SOFA 합동위에서 2020년 10월 19왼 서릅되고 승외되었다. Pursuant to Article Ⅵ, Paragraph 2 of the Agreement under Article Ⅳ of the Mutual Defense Treaty between the Republic of Korea and the United States of America, Regarding Facilities and Areas and the Status of United States Armed Forces in the Republic of Korea, The SOFA Utilities Subcommittee agreed to the Gyeongsangbuk Province’s request for LNG consumer rate change for U.S. Forces Installations in Gyeongsangbuk Province.

(Unit: Won/MJ, exclusive of V.A.T.)

The agreement was signed and approved by the U.S.-ROK SOFA Joint Committee on 19 October, 2020.

1. 사업개요

ㅇ 사업명 : 수도권광역급행철도 B노선 민간투자사업
ㅇ 사업구간 및 규모

- 설계 및 시공 범위 · 본 사업 신설구간 : 인천광역시 인천대입구역 ~ 서울특별시 용산역(약 40km) · 기존선 공용구간 : 서울특별시 상봉역(경춘선 접속부) ~ 경기도 마석역(약 23km) 운영 범위 : 인천대입구역 ~ 마석역

ㅇ 추정 총사업비 : 38,421억원(2020년 12월 31일 기준 불변가격)
ㅇ 공사기간 : 공사착수일로부터 72개월(용지보상 및 시운전기간 포함)

2. 시설사업기본계획 주요 내용

ㅇ 평가방법 및 배점 평가방법 : 2단계로 구분하여 평가하고, 1단계 평가(사전적격성심사)를 통과한 사업신청자에 한

하여 2단계 평가(기술부문, 교통수요부문, 재무부문, 가점 평가) 실시 - 1단계 평가 : 평가항목 및 기준에 따라 출자자의 자격 및 대표자 요건, 재무능력(F), 시공능력 (B), 설계능력(D), 운영능력(O)에 대하여 평가하고, 통과 또는 탈락으로만 구 분하며 2단계 평가 점수와 연계하지 않음(1단계 평가 완료 후 적격자에 한해 개별 통보)

2단계 평가 : 기술부문(550점)과 교통수요부문(150점), 재무부문(300점), 가점(50점)으로 구분하 여 평가를 수행하며, 만점은 가점(50점)을 제외하고 총 1,000점임

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport Notice No. 2022-397

Public Notice on the Designation of the Seoul Metropolitan Area Great Train eXpress Railway Line B as a Public-Private Partnership Project and the Master Plan for the Infrastructure Project Notice is hereby given to announce the designation of the Seoul Metropolitan Area Great Train eXpress (GTX) Railway Line B project as a public-private partnership infrastructure project under Article 8-2 of the Act on Public-Private Partnerships in Infrastructure (hereinafter "PPP Act") and the Master Plan on this infrastructure project under Article 10 of the PPP Act and Article 10 of the Enforcement Decree of the same Act, and make a Request for Proposals for this infrastructure project.

July 04, 2022 Minister of Land, Infrastructure and Transport

1. Overview

ㅇ Project Title : The Seoul Metropolitan Area Great Train eXpress (GTX) Railway Line B Public-Private Partnership Infrastructure Project
ㅇ Project Section and Scope Design and Construction Sections:

Newly Constructed Section: Incheon National University Station, Incheon ~ Yongsan Station, Seoul (approx. 40km) Joint-use Section of Existing Line: Sangbong Station, Seoul (Gyeongchun Line transition zone) ~ Maseok Station, Seoul (approx. 23km)

- Operation Area : Incheon National University Station ~ Maseok Station

ㅇ Estimated Budget : KRW 3,842.1 billion (at constant prices at the end of 2020)
ㅇ Construction Period : Seventy-two (72) months from the commencement date of the construction work (land compensation and test-run period included)

2. Main Contents of the Master Plan for the Infrastructure Project

ㅇ Project Type Under Subparagraph 1 Article 4 of the PPP Act and Article 3 (1) 1 of the Master Plan for

Public-Private Partnership Projects, this infrastructure project shall be conducted in the form of Build-Transfer-Operate (BTO), in which a private sector business entity carries out the construction with private funds and retrieves investment after the construction through operating revenue.

ㅇ Requirements for Project Applicants

A corporate entity which either exists or will be established

- In case where a project applicant is a consortium that consists of five (5) investors or more, the aggregate percentage of shares of the top three (3) major investors shall be equal to or greater than 50% and the percentage of shares of the one (1) largest investor shall be equal to or greater than 25%.
- One consortium cannot have six (6) or more construction companies which are in the list of the top ten (10) constructors in the engineering and construction section of the 2021 comprehensive construction capacity evaluation by the Construction Association of Korea.

ㅇ Concession Term: The concessionaire shall retain the right of operation and maintenance for 40 years from the commencement date of operation.
ㅇ Proposal Evaluation and Scoring

- Evaluation Method: The evaluation process comprises of two phases. The phase 2 evaluation (technical, traffic demand, financial assessments, and additional points) will be conducted only for project applicants who passed the phase 1 evaluation (pre-qualification).
- Phase 1: Investors' qualification, requirements of the representative, financial capability (F), building capability (B), design capability (D) and operation capability (O) will be evaluated based on evaluation criteria and standards. This phase only determines whether a particular applicant has passed or failed, and the result shall not be reflected on the phase 2 evaluation scores (after the completion of the phase 1 evaluation, only successful applicants will be individually notified).
- Phase 2: Evaluation will be done on technical capacity (550 points), traffic demand (150 points), and financial capacity (300 points), making the full score 1000 points. Additional points of up to 50 points may be added.

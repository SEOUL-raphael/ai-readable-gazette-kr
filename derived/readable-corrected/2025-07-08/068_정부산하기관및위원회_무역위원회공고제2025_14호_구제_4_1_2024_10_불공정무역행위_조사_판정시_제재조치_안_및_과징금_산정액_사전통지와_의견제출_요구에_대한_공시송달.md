## 본문

◉무역위원회공고 제2025-14호 행정절차법 제14조 제4항의 규정에 의거 다음과 같이 공시송달합니다.

2025년 07월 08일 무역위원회위원장

구제 4-1-2024-10 불공정무역행위 조사 판정시 제재조치(안) 및 과징금 산정액 사전통지와 의견제출 요구에 대한 공시송달

불공정무역행위 조사 및 산업피해 구제에 관한 법률 제10조(시정조치), 제11조(과징금), 동법 시행 령 제6조(과징금의 산정방법), 제7조(과징금의 부과기준) 및 행정절차법 제21조(처분의 사전통지)에 의거「제재처분 사전통지」공문을 등기우편으로 발송하였으며, 행정절차법 제14조 제4항에 따라 공 시송달을 실시합니다.

ㅇ 공고기간 : 2025. 07. 08. ~ 07. 22. (2주간)
ㅇ 제재처분 대상자 및 처분(안)
ㅇ 무역위원회가 2025. 1. 3.로 조사개시한 「낚시용 집게 디자인권 침해」불공정무역행위 조사 (조사번호 : 구제 4-1-2024-10) 관련입니다.
ㅇ 무역위원회는 동 조사와 관련하여 불공정무역행위에 해당한다고 판정하는 경우 취할 수 있는 모든 제재조치(안)과 그 중 과징금 부과(안) 관련 산정한 과징금을 붙임과 같이 사전에 통지합니다.
ㅇ 이 중 과징금 산정액 등 과징금 부과(안)에 대하여 의견이 있으면 2025.7.22.(화)까지 무역위원 회로 제출하시기 바랍니다.

RE : Investigation of Unfair International Trade Practices regarding Fish Lip Gripper by the Korea Trade Commission (Investigation Number : Remedy 4-1-2024-10)

PRIOR NOTICE OF REQUIRED CORRECTIVE MEASURES AND A PROVISIONAL PENALTY SURCHARGE IN THE EVENT OF A JUDGMENT REGARDING UNFAIR INTERNATIONAL TRADE PRACTICES

Please be advised that this notice is issued in accordance with Articles 21 (Prior Notice of Disposition) and 22 (Seeking Opinions) of the Administrative Procedures Act.

Notice is hereby given that an application was filed with the Korea Trade Commission (hereinafter referred to as “the KTC”) on October 22, 2024, under the Act on the Investigation of Unfair International Trade Practices and Remedy Against Injury to Industry (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and the Enforcement Decree of the Act on behalf of Shimano Inc.

The application alleges the occurrence of violations of the Act based on the supply of fish lip gripper (LFFG-02) into the Republic of Korea from overseas, due to an infringement of the Korean Registered Design No. 30-1105697.

(The application alleges that the supply of the Fish Lip Gripper (LFFG-02) into the Republic of Korea from overseas infringes Korean Registered Design No. 30-1105697, thereby violating Article 4 of the Act.)

Having considered the application (Upon review of the application), the KTC, on January 3, 2025, in accordance with Article 5 of the Act, decided to initiate an investigation to determine whether a violation of Article 4 of the Act occurred regarding supply of fish lip gripper (LFFG-02) into the Republic of Korea from overseas that infringes the Korean Registered Design No. 30-1105697. This decision was duly notified to the relevant parties.

For the purpose of the initiated investigation, the following are hereby named as parties:

(1) The applicant is : Shimano Inc. 3-77 Oimatsu-cho, Sakai-ku, Sakai City, Osaka 590-8577, Japan
(2) The respondent is the following entity alleged to be in violation of the Act and is the party upon which the application is to be served: Zhangjiagang Handtop Outdoor Products Co., Ltd. Changjia Village, Hexing, Jingfeng Town, Zhangjiagang City, Jiangsu Province, China

The KTC hereby provides prior notice of all required corrective measures and a provisional penalty surcharge that may be taken or imposed when the KTC determines that the respondents’ act constitutes a violation of intellectual property rights under Article 4(1) of the Act.

If the KTC determines that the respondents' act constitutes a violation of relevant intellectual property rights, it may take the following corrective measures including, but not limited to:

o suspending the export, import, manufacture or sale of goods;
o prohibiting the entry of goods or requiring the abandonment of such goods;
o ordering corrective advertising and requiring the publication of the corrective order received (Article 10 of the Act).

In the event that a penalty surcharge is imposed, the KTC may levy a surcharge of up to 10% of the total transaction amount over the three years prior to the decision to initiate the investigation.

In cases where there is no transaction amount or calculating the transaction amount is difficult, the penalty surcharge may be set at up to, but not exceed, KRW 500 million (Article 11 of the Act).

The respondent may submit written opinions or relevant materials concerning the required corrective measures or a provisional penalty surcharge to the KTC by Tuesday, July 22, 2025.
